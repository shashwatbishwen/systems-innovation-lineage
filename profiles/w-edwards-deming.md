## Core Systems Takeaway

> "Over 94% of production failures stem from flawed systemic processes, not worker negligence; punishing individuals rather than fixing systemic processes guarantees recurring outages."

**World-Changing Impact:** Pioneered modern industrial quality management, transformed post-war Japanese manufacturing, and created the foundations of Lean and DevOps.

***

## The Architectural Breakthrough

In the 1950s, American statistician W. Edwards Deming lectured post-war Japanese industrialists on Statistical Process Control (SPC). Deming demonstrated that inspecting products for defects at the end of the factory line was expensive waste. Instead, engineers had to track statistical variance at each upstream stage of manufacturing.

Deming articulated that blaming individual operators for defects is destructive: 94% of operational problems belong to the system (bad materials, unstable machinery, conflicting management incentives), while only 6% are attributable to special cause individual mistakes. His Plan-Do-Study-Act (PDSA) cycle institutionalized continuous iterative refinement.
