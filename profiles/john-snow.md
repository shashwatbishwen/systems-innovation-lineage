## Core Systems Takeaway

> "When an organization blames vague environmental factors (miasma) for recurring failures, plot the spatial coordinates to isolate the single corrupt upstream source."

**World-Changing Impact:** Founded modern epidemiology, disproved the airborne miasma theory, and proved waterborne disease transmission.

***

## The Architectural Breakthrough

During the devastating August 1854 cholera outbreak in London Soho district, prevailing medical dogma attributed the disease to airborne miasma (foul air). Anesthesiologist Dr. John Snow rejected this unprovable hypothesis and applied empirical spatial telemetry.

Snow plotted individual cholera deaths as bar charts directly onto a street map of Soho. The visual concentration pointed unmistakably to the Broad Street water pump. Snow interviewed residents, established that workers at a nearby brewery with a private well had zero infections, and had the pump handle removed, terminating the outbreak.
