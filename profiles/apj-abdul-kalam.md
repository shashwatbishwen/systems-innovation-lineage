## Core Systems Takeaway

> "Leadership is absorbing blame when an experimental launch fails, while passing full public credit to the engineering team when the system succeeds."

**World-Changing Impact:** Missile Man of India and 11th President of India; led indigenous solid-propulsion space launch and strategic missile defense architectures.

---

## The Architectural Breakthrough

As Project Director of SLV-III at ISRO in the 1970s, Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam led the design and integration of India first satellite launch vehicle, successfully placing the Rohini satellite into low Earth orbit in July 1980 after a high-profile first launch failure in 1979.

Kalam subsequently directed the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP) at DRDO, coordinating public laboratories, academic institutions, and private industry consortia to build the Agni and Prithvi strategic deterrence platforms under strict international technology denial regimes.

---

## Modern Systems Relevance

Kalam systems management methodology demonstrates how to lead multidisciplinary engineering consortia across disparate domains (aerodynamics, propulsion, guidance avionics, and composite materials) while maintaining unshakeable psychological resilience after initial production failures.

## Attribution & Historical Context

Kalam served as Project Director for India first Satellite Launch Vehicle (SLV-III) at ISRO and later directed DRDO missile development programs (Agni, Prithvi).
