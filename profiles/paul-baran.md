## Core Systems Takeaway

> "Centralized and decentralized networks possess systemic failure nodes; only fully distributed mesh topologies can survive localized component destruction."

**World-Changing Impact:** Conceived survivable decentralized communication networks without centralized vulnerabilities, establishing the blueprint for the ARPANET and Internet.

***

## The Architectural Breakthrough

In 1964 at the RAND Corporation, Paul Baran published On Distributed Communications, commissioned by the US Air Force to design a command network capable of surviving nuclear attack. Baran analyzed three network topologies: centralized (star), decentralized (multi-star), and distributed (mesh).

He demonstrated that centralized networks fail completely when the central hub is severed. Baran introduced message blocks: breaking data into small, self-contained units carrying destination addresses that could navigate dynamically across alternative paths through uncoordinated intermediate nodes.
